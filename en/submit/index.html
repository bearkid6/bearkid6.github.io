tougao@luluh.top Please use the subject line “Submission: your title”

What we publish

Qiushi Papers (求史) is an independently run series of working papers and research reports. Its motto is “Tested on History”. Every article does one thing: it takes a specific claim and tests it against historical data. Any field is welcome, including economics, finance, investing and history. Finding that a claim does not hold is an equally good result.

Articles in Qiushi Papers are not peer reviewed. It has no ISSN and is not a formally published journal.

Requirements

  • Original work that has not been formally published elsewhere.
  • State where the data come from. Including the data and code is even better.
  • Chinese or English.
  • Send the manuscript as PDF or Word (.docx). Figures and data tables may be attached. Keep each email under 20 MB.
  • Do not send .exe, .zip or similar files. The inbox does not keep them.

After acceptance

  • The article carries your name. A pen name is fine.
  • It is published under CC BY 4.0: anyone may share and adapt it with attribution.
  • Authors are responsible for their own content.
  • Qiushi Papers typesets the web and PDF versions, and you approve them before they go online.

Process

  1. Email your manuscript to tougao@luluh.top.
  2. The editor reads it.
  3. If we decide to publish it, we will contact you about revisions and typesetting.

Your email address is used only to contact you and is never made public.
